How to read these results

Audit complete; model calibration remains open

All 55 samples completed a full 3,600-second profiling window after warmup. Checks cover replay configuration, dependency structure, tokenizer agreement, request accounting, timeouts and worker placement. Agg also checks replay inputs against the saved hardware runs.

The agg curve contains eight matched policy/concurrency samples; RR at 192 and 384 reuses the capacity-control runs. The disagg curve contains all 47 samples from the 64-GPU topology sweep. Agg and disagg use different cache-capacity assumptions. The fixed-rate service and approximate cache models still have known hardware gaps; hybrid/Mamba state and shared GPU scheduling are not modeled here.
